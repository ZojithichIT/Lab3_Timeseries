[Question 3] When Is XGBoost More Suitable Than ARIMA / SARIMA?
  - Choose XGBoost when: Large high-frequency series (N > 5,000), multiple nested seasonalities (24h + 168h + annual),
    strong non-linear regime shifts, and exogenous features (weather, holidays) are available.
  - Choose ARIMA/SARIMA when: Short univariate series (N < 500), low frequency (monthly/quarterly), linear trend
    extrapolation beyond historical min/max range is required (trees cannot extrapolate beyond leaf bounds without differencing).
